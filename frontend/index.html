Investing vs Trading

Protect your wealth by understanding the difference.

Investing (Safe, Long-Term)

  • Focuses on building wealth gradually over years or decades.
  • Relies on fundamental analysis of a company's true value.
  • Lower risk, requires patience, and ignores short-term market noise.

Trading (High Risk, Short-Term)

  • Focuses on making quick profits from short-term price fluctuations.
  • Highly stressful, requires constant monitoring of technical charts.
  • Most retail traders lose money. Scammers target people looking for "quick trading profits".
Golden Rule: If someone guarantees you quick profits through trading, it is almost certainly a scam. True wealth is built through patient investing.
